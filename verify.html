Verify and update GTA V

Broken or old game files make FiveM and shaders crash. Use Steam or Rockstar to repair and update.

Verify on Steam for me Open Rockstar Launcher Open GTA5.exe folder

Windows may show SmartScreen on the helper. The Steam helper only asks Steam to validate Grand Theft Auto V (app 271590). The Rockstar helper only opens the official launcher.

STEAM

Open GTA V properties

  1. Open Steam.
  2. Go to Library.
  3. Right-click Grand Theft Auto V.
  4. Click Properties.
Open GTA V in Steam
STEAM

Verify integrity of game files

  1. In Properties, open Installed Files.
  2. Click Verify integrity of game files.
  3. Wait until Steam finishes. It may re-download missing files.
Do it for me — Steam verify
STEAM

Update GTA V on Steam

  1. Open your Library and select Grand Theft Auto V.
  2. If you see Update, click it and wait.
  3. Steam also updates the game when you verify files or click Play.
Open Steam library page
ROCKSTAR

Verify and update in Rockstar Launcher

  1. Open Rockstar Games Launcher and sign in.
  2. Select Grand Theft Auto V.
  3. Open the game settings / extra options.
  4. Click Verify game files or Repair.
  5. If an update is available, click Update first.
Open Rockstar for me Show GTA5.exe

Rockstar does not offer a one-click verify link like Steam. The helper opens the launcher. You still click Verify / Update there.
